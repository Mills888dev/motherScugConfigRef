# Train: the spear

| Setting | Default | Unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spear_enabled` | `false` |  | Enables fetching, carrying and throwing spears. |
| `spear_throw_dist_px` | `160.0` | px | Farthest distance to the player at which they throw a spear. |
| `spear_throw_speed` | `35.0` | px/tick | Speed of a thrown spear. |
| `spear_damage` | `1.0` | game damage units | Damage of a thrown spear. The game's own spear does 1. |
| `spear_throw_cooldown_ticks` | `40` | ticks | Wait after a throw before the next throw. |
