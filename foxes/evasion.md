# Cherry: the dodge

| Setting | Default | Unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `evasion_enabled` | `false` |  | Enables dodging thrown spears. |
| `evasion_chance` | `0.6` | 0-1 | Chance they dodge a spear. |
| `evasion_cooldown_ticks` | `40` | ticks | Wait after a dodge before the next dodge. |
