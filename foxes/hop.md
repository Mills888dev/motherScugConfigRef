# Potato: the hop

| Setting | Default | Unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hop_enabled` | `false` |  | Enables hopping while travelling, like a Cyan lizard. |
| `hop_when` | `"moving"` |  | When they may hop. Options: moving, hunting, hurry. |
| `hop_speed_fac` | `1.0` | times full leap speed | Hop speed compared with a full pounce. |
| `hop_cooldown_ticks` | `20` | ticks | Wait after a hop before the next hop. |
